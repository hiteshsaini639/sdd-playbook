# W1 — After launch: getting users and keeping them

Status: todo · Area: practices · Parked: 2026-11-17 (owner)

A practice for the funnel once the product is live: who signs up, who
reaches the first real use, who keeps using it, and what to build for each step.
- Count the funnel from production before building anything for it. Take
  each step from sign-up to the habit and the share of users that reaches
  it. One project's first count found that most users stalled after
  sign-up, at the first real action, and not at sign-up itself.
- Keep users before getting more. New users fall into the same gap as the
  ones you already have.
- Make the counts a product feature, such as an admin page, and not a
  one-off query. Then the next count is cheap. Count again a fixed time
  after each change.
- To bring users in, a link handed over by someone the user already
  trusts (an invite or a QR code) beats advertising.
- Questions only the domain expert can answer go to them together, in one
  message.

Earns its place when: a project has counted before and after a change and
seen whether it moved the number.
